What a typical engagement looks like.

Three phases / One engagement

  1. 01Days 1–3

    Audit and align.

    A free audit of your site, your competitors and how you show up on Google and in AI answers. Then a plan you approve before anything is built.

  2. 02Days 4–14

    Build and launch.

    A mockup, your feedback, then the build: pages, schema markup, Google Business Profile and analytics. Live in about two weeks.

  3. 03Day 30 on

    Report and grow.

    Your first monthly report card at day 30. Then content, rank tracking, AI-answer monitoring and fixes, every month.

What we won’t promise.

Straight answers / No surprises

Things you’ll hear from other agencies that you won’t hear from us:

A #1 ranking guarantee.

Nobody controls Google’s rankings. Anyone promising the top spot is guessing, or gaming it in ways that get sites penalized.

Results in 30 days.

Search compounds over months. You’ll see the work and the early signals in your monthly report card, without the hype.

Traffic for traffic’s sake.

We measure calls, bookings and rentals. A spike of visitors who never become customers isn’t a result.
