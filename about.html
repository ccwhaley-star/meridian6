The Name

Why the name. The Sixth Principal Meridian.

In 1855, surveyors fixed a line on the Kansas prairie: the Sixth Principal Meridian. Every township, every section, every acre across Kansas and much of the Midwest is still measured from that one reference line — a single fixed truth the whole map is built on.

That’s the standard we hold ourselves to. Small businesses don’t need more noise about AI and algorithms — they need a fixed point: what’s true, what’s measured, and what actually brings customers in the door.

Fixed

One reference point for your whole digital presence — site, search, ads, AI.

Measured

Rankings, calls, and revenue you can count. Never vibes.

On the grid

Built section by section, from first principles — nothing off template.

The Operator

Meet the operator behind Meridian6.

After a career in enterprise technology, Chris spent the last 10 years working for private equity firms and digital agencies. Then he started his own small business, acquiring and operating self-storage facilities across the Midwest. He needed websites that ranked, Google Ads that converted, and a way to tell his story online that local agencies struggled to provide. So he built everything himself.
