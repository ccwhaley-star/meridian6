Google Business Profile: The Free Marketing Tool Most Small Businesses Ignore

← Insights  ·  Meridian6  ·  4 min read

The highest-leverage marketing asset most small businesses own is one they never log into. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you appear in the map results — the block that sits above every regular listing for local searches — and it costs exactly nothing.

Why it outranks your website

For “near me” and “[service] + [city]” searches, Google shows the map pack first. Three businesses. Phone numbers, reviews, directions — a customer can choose without ever reaching the actual results. If you're not in that box, page one of the regular results is a consolation prize.

The complete afternoon pass

  1. Claim and verify it — google.com/business. If a profile exists that you didn't make, claim it before someone's outdated info keeps costing you calls.
  2. Fill every field. Hours, services, service area, description, attributes. Completeness is a ranking factor and half-finished profiles are the norm — being thorough is a competitive act.
  3. Choose categories precisely. Your primary category is the strongest signal on the profile. “Website designer” and “Marketing agency” are different shelves; be on the right one, then add accurate secondaries.
  4. Match your website exactly. Name, phone, service area — identical to your site, character for character. Google cross-checks; so do AI assistants.
  5. Add real photos. Profiles with genuine photos get dramatically more engagement than logo-only ghosts. Your work, your team, your space.
  6. Build the review habit. A short link sent after every job, and a reply to every review. This is the compounding asset — start now, thank yourself in a year.

The maintenance reality

A GBP isn't set-and-forget: hours change, services evolve, questions arrive, reviews need answers. It's twenty minutes a month — or it's part of what a growth retainer handles for you, which is exactly why GBP optimization is baked into our SEO service.
